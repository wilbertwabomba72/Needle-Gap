Simple Rules, Sharp Timing

Tap to fire a pin. Hit an open gap. Miss the pins already attached to the rotating core. Needle Gap keeps the controls simple and lets speed, spacing, and rhythm create the challenge.

50 Levels A complete offline campaign with steadily increasing difficulty.
One-Tap Play Built for quick sessions, clean input, and immediate retries.
Local Progress Level unlocks, stars, and settings are stored on the device.
No Distractions No login, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no network features.
