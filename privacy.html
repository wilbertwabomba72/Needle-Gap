Needle Gap
Game Overview

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

Needle Gap is an offline game. We do not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

Information We Collect

Needle Gap does not collect personal information. The game does not require an account, email address, phone number, name, location, photos, contacts, or any other personal data.

Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device, including unlocked levels, star ratings, music settings, and sound settings. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party.

Network, Ads, and Tracking

Needle Gap does not use network features, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or third-party login services. The app does not track you across apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

Needle Gap does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Because the app is offline and does not collect data, no personal information is requested from players.

Data Sharing

We do not share personal data because Needle Gap does not collect personal data.

Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored progress by using the reset progress option in the app settings, or by deleting the app from your device. Deleting the app removes the local data stored by the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
